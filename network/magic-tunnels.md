# Crossing registry: proven engineering vs. magic tunnels

Per notes/002: politics and cost are ignored, engineering conservatism is
not. Every major crossing gets either a **proven-tech justification**
(a real project of comparable difficulty exists) or an explicit **MAGIC**
declaration (feasibility would require geological study we refuse to do).
Magic is allowed for core network links but never for gateway branches.

## Tier 3 (HSR)

| Crossing | Verdict | Precedent / note |
|---|---|---|
| Pacheco Pass tunnel (SJ–Central Valley, ~25 km) | PROVEN | CAHSR's own engineering studies; shorter than Gotthard (57 km) |
| Grapevine/Tejon base tunnel (Bakersfield–LA, ~40–50 km total tunneling) | PROVEN-ish, flagged | Gotthard-class lengths; crosses the San Andreas & Garlock faults — real projects cross faults with enlarged vaults (as CAHSR's Tehachapi plans do), but alignment specifics here are hand-waved. Call it proven-with-asterisk. |
| Second Transbay tube (SF–Oakland, ~10 km immersed/bored) | PROVEN | Marmaray (Bosphorus), Chesapeake tunnels; a second tube is long-studied by BART |
| San Gabriel Valley approaches (LA–Ontario) | PROVEN | Surface/viaduct + short tunnels |
| Escondido–San Diego (~15 km tunnel through mesas) | PROVEN | Standard bored tunnel geology |

## Tier 1 (urban)

All urban tunnels (Twin Peaks, Mt Sutro, Mt Davidson, Corona Heights,
Sepulveda Pass, Laurel Canyon, Golden Gate Park undercrossings) are
routine bored metro tunnels — PROVEN, no entries needed.
