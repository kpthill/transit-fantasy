# 003 — Network sketch (spitball draft, pre-data)

Status: DRAFT — Claude's back-of-envelope application of the 002 heuristics,
written for a shared-vision check with Kevin before the data-driven pass.
Numbers are estimates from general knowledge, not computed. The real network
build will re-derive all of this from Census/LODES/OSM data; where the data
disagrees with this sketch, the data wins (but flag big surprises).

## Full 1-km grids (~1,300–1,700 km² statewide, ~8–10M people in walkshed)

- **San Francisco**: entire city (~120 km²). Flagship worked example.
- **Bay Area**: Berkeley + Oakland flats (downtown–Fruitvale), Daly City,
  central San Jose patch only (citywide SJ is medium-density). ~200–300 km².
- **LA basin** (~700–900 km²): one contiguous grid — Valley cores (Van Nuys/
  Panorama City), Hollywood, Koreatown, downtown, East LA, South LA,
  southeast cities (Huntington Park/Bell/South Gate), Santa Ana, central
  Long Beach; pockets: Glendale, Pasadena core, Inglewood.
- **San Diego**: downtown–Hillcrest–North Park–City Heights (~100–150 km²).
- **Small patches**: downtown/Midtown Sacramento, central Fresno, downtown
  Santa Barbara.

## Corridors + highlights (most of urban CA by area)

San Gabriel + outer San Fernando Valleys, Orange County (Disneyland/Anaheim
highlight cluster), Inland Empire, Silicon Valley (El Camino Real + Stevens
Creek lines; spurs to Google/Apple/Stanford), East Bay suburbs,
Sacramento/San Diego sprawl, bulk of Fresno/Bakersfield/Stockton.
Highlights: Disneyland, Universal, SoFi/Dodger/Rose Bowl, UC campuses
(UCLA, UCB, UCSD, UCI, UCD, UCSB/Isla Vista), Santa Monica–Huntington
beaches, fairgrounds, convention centers.

## Tier 2 (~4,000–5,000 route-km)

- **Bay Area web** (~900 km): SF–SJ Peninsula; East Bay ring via
  Oakland–Fremont; Concord–Antioch; Marin–Santa Rosa;
  Vallejo–Fairfield–Sacramento; SJ–Gilroy–Salinas/Monterey;
  Livermore–Tracy–Stockton (Altamont).
- **LA web** (~1,100 km): Ventura/Santa Barbara; Santa Clarita–Palmdale–
  Lancaster; San Bernardino–Redlands; Riverside–Palm Springs–Coachella;
  OC coast–Oceanside–San Diego; Victorville–Barstow (Cajon).
- **San Diego web** (~250 km): coastal; Escondido; El Cajon; San Ysidro/
  Tijuana border station.
- **Central Valley spine**: Sac–Stockton–Modesto–Merced–Fresno–Visalia–
  Bakersfield, all 15k+ towns; north to Chico–Redding.
- **Coast route**: Gilroy–Salinas–Paso Robles–SLO–Santa Maria–Santa
  Barbara–Ventura; Santa Cruz–Watsonville. 200 km/h segments, no HSR.
- **Mountain/desert**: Sac–Auburn–Truckee/Tahoe (Donner — existing rail,
  no magic); Bakersfield–Mojave–Lancaster; Coachella–El Centro–Calexico.
- **Gateway branches** (per 002 gateway rule, ≥ ~1M visitors + feasible
  corridor):
  - **South Lake Tahoe**: Sacramento–Placerville–South Lake Tahoe (US-50
    corridor); north shore already reached via Truckee/Donner.
  - **Yosemite**: Merced–Mariposa–El Portal on the historical Yosemite
    Valley RR alignment; shuttle into the Valley.
  - **Sequoia/Kings Canyon**: Visalia–Three Rivers gateway; the Generals
    Highway climb stays road/shuttle.
  - **Napa/Sonoma wine country**: Vallejo–Napa–St. Helena–Calistoga branch
    (rail existed; Wine Train survives), plus Santa Rosa–Sonoma spur.
  - **Joshua Tree**: modest desert branch or stop off the Coachella line
    (Palm Springs side gateway); easy terrain, borderline — lean yes.
  - **Rejected/flagged no**: Death Valley (too remote, visitation
    marginal), Lassen (~500k visitors, below bar), Big Sur (terrain,
    keep Monterey/Carmel on coast line), Big Bear (7,000 ft climb, no
    precedent — geological-study territory), Mammoth/US-395 (nothing to
    chain a 250 km isolated mountain line to).
- **Flagged judgment calls**: Eureka line (weakest corridor in the state;
  leaning yes on 50-years-of-investment grounds).

## Tier 3 HSR (~1,300–1,500 km): backbone + three limbs, not a mesh

- **Spine**: SF Transbay–SJ–Fresno–Kings/Tulare–Bakersfield–LA Union.
  Pacheco tunnel; **Grapevine/Tehachapi base tunnel** instead of CAHSR's
  Palmdale dogleg (dogleg is political; Palmdale gets tier-2).
  SF–LA ≈ 2h05–2h20.
- **North limb**: second Transbay tube, SF–Oakland–Sacramento (~45 min);
  valley link Sac–Stockton–Modesto–Merced joining the spine.
- **South limb**: LA–Ontario–Riverside–Escondido–San Diego (inland; coast
  stays tier-2); LA–Anaheim stub. SF–SD ≈ 3h.
- **Explicitly not HSR**: coast route, Santa Rosa (Golden Gate tunnel not
  justified at ~500k metro), far north.

## Outside the big three metros

Sacramento = fourth near-full citizen (small grid, corridors, web, HSR both
directions). Valley cities: corridors + spine + HSR stops. Chico/Redding:
tier-2 with in-town stations. SLO/Monterey/Santa Barbara: coast line +
corridors. Palm Springs/Victorville/El Centro: tier-2 branches. Uncovered:
rural Sierra, US-395 corridor, far northeast (Susanville, Alturas), North
Coast pending the Eureka call — taxi/drive-to-station country.

## Vision in one sentence

Rail you can walk to in the dense quarter of urban California; rail you can
taxi to in ten minutes nearly everywhere people actually live; SF–LA faster
than flying door-to-door.
