## Done (2026-08-05)

- **Trunk-merge pass** (in SF grid generator, will generalize): where two
  lines run sustained-parallel closer than ~240 m for > 500 m, the shorter
  snaps onto the longer — shared trunk, shared stations, like real
  interlining. Crossings (single-point proximity) untouched. In SF this
  merged: Polk onto the Van Ness trunk, Stockton/Columbus North Beach
  approaches, and the West Portal approaches of the two hill tunnels.
  Deliberately NOT merged: pairs ~350–450 m apart downtown (Stockton vs
  Kearny/Columbus) — parallel CBD trunks at that spacing are realistic.
- **Statewide tier-2**, hand-authored station lists, straight-segment
  geometry (tracing later): LA web (7 lines: Coast to Goleta, Antelope
  Valley, San Bernardino, Coachella, Orange County, Cajon, Long Beach),
  San Diego web (4 lines incl. San Ysidro border), Central Valley spine +
  North Valley to Redding + both Tahoe branches + Tehachapi link, Central
  Coast + Monterey + Santa Cruz, Wine Country + Sonoma + Redwood/Eureka
  line, gateway branches (Imperial/Calexico, Yosemite via Mariposa,
  Sequoia/Three Rivers, Joshua Tree).
  Judgment calls: Eureka built (lean-yes per 003, 60-min headway — the
  network's sparsest service); Tahoe gets BOTH north (Donner/Truckee) and
  south (Placerville) branches per gateway rule; Coachella line shares the
  LA–Ontario trunk with the San Bernardino line.

- **Multi-city generator**: build_city_grid.py + pipeline/cities.py
  per-city configs. East Bay (Oakland–Berkeley) grid shipped: 11
  corridors / 85 stations, hub-integrated at Oakland Center. Hub-bend
  rewritten to insert by arc position (bug found via East Bay terminus).

- **All nine city grids shipped** (2026-08-05): SF, East Bay, LA basin
  (34 corridors / 654 stations), San Diego, San Jose, Sacramento,
  Fresno, Anaheim–Santa Ana (Disneyland at Harbor & Katella), Long
  Beach. 118 lines / ~1,470 stations; all pass zero-spike/zero-dangle.
  Generator handles directional street-name prefixes generically.
- **Station naming pass**: 935/1106 placeholders renamed to
  "Corridor & Cross Street" via per-city Overpass lookups
  (pipeline/name_stations.py). Remainder are mid-tunnel/park stations —
  neighborhood-based names would need a places dataset; deferred.
- **Crossing registry** (network/magic-tunnels.md): every major crossing
